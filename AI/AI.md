# AI (Artificial Intelligence)

## ANI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)
- Only able to perform a specific job
- smart speakers, self-driving cars, web search, AI in factories

## Generative AI
- Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content, such as text, images, music, videos, computer code, or even 3D designs—based on patterns it has learned from large amounts of existing data.
- Eg. ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude

## AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)
- Do anything a human can do

`AI has boomed now due to the rise of neural network, large neural network ~ better performance`
